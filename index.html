            Stall guide

            Two ways to build a stall.

            A

            Octanorm, or shell scheme

            A modular aluminium frame with printed panels, a fascia name board, a counter and lights. Quick to put up and easy on the budget, it suits shorter shows and multiple stalls in one hall.

            • Fastest to install
            • Lower cost per square metre
            • Branding by printed panels
            B

            Custom fabrication

            A stand designed for one brand and built for one show, with shaped fascias, lit linework, meeting lounges and display walls. It starts from a 3D visual and takes more time, but it owns the aisle.

            • Shaped to the brand
            • Built-in lighting and lounges
            • Reviewed in 3D first
